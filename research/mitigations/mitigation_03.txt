mitigation_category: idv_hardening
control_name: behavioral_and_device_signals
summary: Combine identity verification with behavioral and device telemetry to reduce fraud exposure and improve confidence in a live user session.

why_it_matters:
- Device and behavioral signals add context beyond static identity documents or a single biometric check.
- Public fraud guidance recommends layered controls because single signals are easier to spoof or replay.

related_risks:
- synthetic_media
- liveness_bypass
- session_abuse
- document_fraud

public_sources:
- source_type: advisory
  url: https://example.com/public-identity-fraud-advisory
  notes: Public guidance notes that a single verification signal is insufficient in the face of synthetic and manipulated media.
- source_type: research
  url: https://example.com/public-anti-spoofing-research
  notes: Research emphasizes layered controls and context-based analysis to reduce spoofing and abuse.

implementation_notes:
- Combine device signals, session state checks, and behavioral heuristics with biometric or KYC validation.
- Review anomalies or suspicious sessions with secondary escalation.
- Tune thresholds to reduce false positives while preserving security posture.
