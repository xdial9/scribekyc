mitigation_category: onboarding_fraud_controls
control_name: randomized_verification_orchestration
summary: Build onboarding and identity verification around randomized challenge flows, session integrity checks, and structured escalation for suspicious or repeated attempts.

why_it_matters:
- Public guidance repeatedly recommends randomization and session validation because predictable workflows create replay and automation opportunities.
- Fraud resistance improves when verification checks are orchestrated as a system rather than as isolated checks.

related_risks:
- session_abuse
- liveness_bypass
- synthetic_media
- document_fraud

public_sources:
- source_type: research
  url: https://example.com/public-challenge-design-guidance
  notes: Public guidance emphasizes randomized challenge design and validation in identity verification.
- source_type: advisory
  url: https://example.com/public-fraud-escalation-guidance
  notes: Public fraud guidance recommends escalation and secondary review for suspicious or repeated attempts.

implementation_notes:
- Use randomized, session-linked challenges to prevent replay and automation abuse.
- Enforce server-side validation of challenge sequencing and session state.
- Escalate repeated or suspicious attempts to human review or secondary verification.
