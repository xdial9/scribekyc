mitigation_category: idv_hardening
control_name: active_liveness_challenge
summary: Use active, randomized liveness checks to prevent simple replay or static image bypasses during digital identity verification.

why_it_matters:
- Liveness checks are a common front line against synthetic media and replay attacks.
- Weak or passive verification systems are vulnerable to presentation and deepfake attacks.

related_risks:
- liveness_bypass
- synthetic_media
- session_abuse
- document_fraud

implementation_notes:
- Randomize prompts and challenge sequences.
- Combine active verification with image quality and anti-spoofing models.
- Escalate suspicious or failed verification attempts to secondary review.
