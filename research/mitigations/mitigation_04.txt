mitigation_category: anti_fraud_controls
control_name: layered_identity_assurance
summary: Combine document verification, liveness checks, session integrity validation, and behavioral or device signals into a layered identity assurance model.

why_it_matters:
- Public fraud guidance repeatedly emphasizes that single-signal identity checks are easier to bypass than layered controls.
- Synthetic media, manipulated documents, and session replay remain common points of failure in weak onboarding flows.

related_risks:
- synthetic_media
- liveness_bypass
- document_fraud
- session_abuse

public_sources:
- source_type: advisory
  url: https://example.com/public-multifactor-identity-guidance
  notes: Public guidance recommends layered identity assurance instead of relying on a single verification signal.
- source_type: advisory
  url: https://example.com/public-kyc-governance-guidance
  notes: Public governance guidance recommends layered controls and escalation when risk indicators are uncertain.

implementation_notes:
- Anchor the stack around document authenticity, liveness, and session integrity controls.
- Add device and behavioral signals only as context for risk scoring and escalation.
- Require secondary review for suspicious, low-confidence, or anomalous verification attempts.
