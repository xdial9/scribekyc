mitigation_category: kyc_best_practices
control_name: document_authenticity_validation
summary: Validate submitted identity documents for authenticity, tampering, and image integrity before allowing onboarding or verification to continue.

why_it_matters:
- Identity verification systems are frequently challenged by manipulated or synthetic document uploads.
- Fraud prevention guidance consistently recommends authenticity checks and anomaly review for suspicious files.

related_risks:
- document_manipulation
- synthetic_media
- document_fraud
- session_abuse

- source_type: advisory
  url: https://example.com/public-document-authenticity-guidance

- source_type: research
  url: https://example.com/public-document-fraud-research
  notes: Research notes the risks of altered and synthetic identity documents in digital onboarding.

implementation_notes:
- Check for image tampering, duplicate uploads, and suspicious artifacts.
- Require additional review for low-confidence or anomalous document files.
- Combine with liveness and behavioral checks for higher assurance.
