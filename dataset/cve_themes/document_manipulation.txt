Risk Theme: Document manipulation
Category: Identity verification / KYC
Description:
Document manipulation involves altering or enhancing identity documents to bypass verification controls through manipulated scans, images, or injected artifacts.

Common Attack Patterns:
- Modified document images
- Fake or altered identity photos
- Reused or manipulated biometric data
- Screenshots or synthetic document images

Impact:
- Fraudulent identity creation
- Onboarding bypass
- Synthetic identity risk
- Increased KYC compliance exposure

Typical Controls:
- Document authenticity validation
- OCR and security feature checks
- Image tamper detection
- Human or secondary review for outliers
- Multi-factor identity checks

This note reflects common public anti-fraud and KYC theme research.
