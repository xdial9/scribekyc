Risk Theme: Session or verification workflow abuse
Category: Identity verification / onboarding
Description:
Verification workflows can be abused when session integrity or workflow controls are insufficient. This may include repeated attempts, manipulated steps, or bypass attempts across the onboarding flow.

Common Attack Patterns:
- Session replay or fixation
- Workflow skip or manipulation
- Repeated attempts with weakening controls
- Abuse of gaps between verification stages

Impact:
- Fraudulent identity acceptance
- Manual review overload
- Abuse of onboarding workflows
- Security and compliance exposure

Typical Controls:
- Session integrity checks
- Step-level validation and logging
- Suspicious pattern detection
- Device and network anomaly review

This note reflects general public security risk themes in verification systems.
