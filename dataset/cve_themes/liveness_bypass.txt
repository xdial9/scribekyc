Risk Theme: Liveness bypass
Category: Identity verification / KYC
Description:
A liveness bypass occurs when an attacker presents synthetic media, replayed footage, or manipulated visuals in a way that defeats biometric verification.

Common Attack Patterns:
- Deepfake face presented to camera
- Replay of a prerecorded video
- Printed photo or masked presentation
- Synthetic media tuned to evade detection

Impact:
- Identity fraud
- Account takeover risk
- Fraudulent onboarding or account creation
- Increased scam and impersonation risk

Typical Controls:
- Active liveness challenge
- Randomized prompt sequence
- Multi-frame challenge validation
- Quality scoring and anti-spoofing checks
- Human review for suspicious sessions

This note reflects common public anti-fraud and identity verification themes.
