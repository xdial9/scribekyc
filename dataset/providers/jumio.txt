Provider: Jumio
Category: Identity Verification / KYC
Product Types:
- KYC onboarding
- Identity verification
- Document scanning
- Liveness checks

Public Notes:
- Jumio is a common identity verification provider used in onboarding and authentication flows.
- Public risk themes include document verification manipulation, deepfake attacks, and session or verification workflow abuse.

Public Risk Themes:
- Document fraud or manipulated uploads
- Liveness defeat via synthetic media
- Session flow abuse or verification bypass patterns
- Weak image or device validation in some implementations

General Mitigation Guidance:
- Attack-resistant liveness checks
- Strong document integrity validation
- Multi-layer verification controls
- Fraud scoring and anomaly detection
- Additional challenge workflows for suspicious sessions

This note is intentionally generalized and public-source based.
