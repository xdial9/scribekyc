Provider: Socure
Category: Identity Verification / KYC
Product Types:
- Identity verification
- Document verification
- Liveness detection
- Risk scoring

Public Notes:
- Socure is widely used in identity verification and onboarding workflows.
- Public security research often focuses on liveness detection, document verification, and synthetic media attacks.
- Legacy or weakly configured verification pipelines may be more exposed to presentation attacks.

Public Risk Themes:
- Liveness bypass via deepfakes or replayed media
- Synthetic media attacks against facial verification
- Document fraud or manipulated upload vectors
- Weak anti-spoofing controls in older implementations

General Mitigation Guidance:
- Active liveness challenge flows
- Anti-spoofing quality checks
- Multi-step verification
- Document authenticity validation
- Risk-based anomaly monitoring

This note is intentionally generalized and public-source based.
