Category: KYC best practices
Description:
KYC and onboarding controls should combine identity verification, document validation, and behavioral signals to reduce fraud.

Recommended Practices:
- Use layered identity verification
- Validate documentation and capture quality
- Check for AI-generated or manipulated media
- Monitor repeated patterns and anomaly signals
- Require secondary review for risky flows
- Keep verification and fraud systems updated

This note reflects general public anti-fraud and compliance guidance in KYC operations.
