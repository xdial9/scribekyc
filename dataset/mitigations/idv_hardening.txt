Category: Identity verification / KYC hardening
Description:
Risk hardening for IDV and KYC systems is a combination of device, document, and biometric controls designed to reduce fraud and spoofing risk.

Key Controls:
- Active liveness verification
- Multi-factor verification
- Device and network anomaly checks
- Document authenticity validation
- Behavioral risk analysis
- Fraud scoring with exception review
- Human review for edge cases

General Guidance:
- Don’t rely on one control alone
- Use layered defenses
- Prioritize anti-spoofing and anomaly detection
- Maintain review and escalation paths for unusual verification attempts

This note summarizes general public guidance for hardening identity verification systems.
