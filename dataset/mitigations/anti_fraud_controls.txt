Category: Anti-fraud controls
Description:
Fraud controls in IDV and KYC workflows typically combine challenge flows, anomaly detection, and manual review.

Examples:
- Liveness challenge prompts
- Biometric quality checks
- Suspicious document detection
- Device fingerprint review
- Behavior-based fraud scoring
- Human review for risk escalations

Why it matters:
- Fraudsters adapt quickly to weak controls
- Synthetic media and manipulated documents are increasingly common
- A single control is rarely sufficient

This note reflects common public anti-fraud patterns.
